**Subject:** Data-sharing inquiry — Odaw Basin drainage network data for an open flood early-warning pilot

Dear GARID team,

My name is Robert Addo-Asante-Darko. I'm the AI engineer on a small team building **ThirdWave**, a flood early-warning platform for Accra — a working pilot covering Accra Metropolis, Korle Klottey, the Ablekuma assemblies, Ayawaso Central, and Weija Gbawe, using only free/open satellite data (Sentinel-1 SAR, Copernicus DEM, ESA WorldCover) and OpenStreetMap. The project is public on GitHub: github.com/ROBERT-ADDO-ASANTE-DARKO/ThirdWave.

Our composite flood-vulnerability score currently combines SAR water occurrence, elevation, and land cover — but it has one structural blind spot we can't close with free data alone: we have no drainage/culvert **capacity** information (pipe diameter, material, condition, connectivity) for the Odaw River Basin, which is where the majority of our documented historical flood events cluster (Kwame Nkrumah Circle, Kaneshie, Alajo, and others). Without that layer, our model can flag a location as chronically wet but can't explain *why* a specific street floods at a specific rainfall threshold, or how a proposed drainage intervention would change that.

We understand GARID has produced drainage system maps for exactly this corridor as part of the flood-risk-management component of the project. We'd like to ask:

1. Is any of GARID's Odaw Basin drainage network data (pipe/culvert geometry, capacity, condition) available for use by an independent civic-tech pilot, even in a limited or aggregated form?
2. If so, what is the process for requesting it — is there a formal data-sharing agreement, or a point of contact who handles this?
3. Are there any restrictions on how the data could be used or published (e.g. attribution requirements, non-commercial use only, specific catchments only)?

We're a non-commercial pilot at this stage, and we'd be glad to credit GARID as a data source, share our resulting risk-scoring outputs back if that's useful to you, and work within whatever terms make sense on your end.

Thank you for your time — happy to answer any questions about the project or share more detail on request.
