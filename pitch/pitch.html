01 — The ProblemData-poor, storm-prone

Accra floods every rainy season. It has almost nothing to see it coming with.

§1

No dense rain-gauge network. No calibrated hydraulic model of the drainage system — because the drainage system itself is largely undocumented. No shared picture between the officials who'd respond and the residents in the water's path. Kwame Nkrumah Circle floods on a predictable basis, and until this project scored it, that pattern lived in institutional memory and news archives, not in any system a duty officer could query.

02 — Data FusionFree, global, real

Three public satellite layers, fused into one explainable score.

§2

Every plate below is real output from this project's pipeline, over the actual pilot district — not a mockup. Sentinel-1 radar sees standing water through cloud cover; Copernicus DEM gives terrain; ESA WorldCover gives surface type. No signup, no cost, no dependency on infrastructure records that don't exist.

SCORE = 0.45·SAR_OCC + 0.35·LOW_ELEV + 0.20·IMPERVIOUS
Transparent by design — every score traces to three numbers a duty officer can audit, not a black box. Land cover was tested as a weighted term and dropped: it flattened every zone toward "Low," so it's kept as disclosed context instead.

05 — Where AI Does The WorkFive specific jobs

Not "AI everywhere." AI where the constraint actually required it.

§5
01

Risk assessment with zero local ground truth

Global ML-classified products (SAR, DEM, land cover) fused into one score, at a resolution fine enough to catch the 2015 Circle disaster a coarser assembly-level score missed entirely.

02

Noisy radar hiding small water bodies

A deep despeckling model (SAR2SAR) cleans Sentinel-1 speckle before water-occurrence is computed — validated against a known area before being trusted in the pipeline.

03

Unstructured citizen photos

A vision-language model turns a submitted flood photo into a structured depth/severity read with bounding-box annotation — the resident validates it before it's submitted.

04

Hydraulic modeling requiring infrastructure data nobody has

A lightweight cellular-automaton proxy over real terrain shows plausible ponding behavior without pretending to be a calibrated hydraulic model.

05

GIS tools locking out non-technical officials

Tool-calling chat over the real computed data — a duty officer asks in plain language, gets an answer grounded in actual zone data, not a hallucination.

”

The core risk score is a transparent formula, not a trained model. We checked whether a public satellite flood-extent dataset could justify a predictive model instead — it couldn't: wrong resolution (250m MODIS) for street-scale urban flooding, and not enough local labeled events to validate one responsibly either way. Knowing where not to force AI is the same engineering discipline as knowing where to use it.

Accumulating now

Citizen reports as a real ground-truth stream

Each geotagged report with a VLM depth estimate is a sparse, real observation — the kind of anchor data a physics-informed model actually needs.

Future direction

Physics-informed neural network (PINN) for surface flow

Once enough citizen-report observations accumulate to anchor it, a shallow-water-equation PINN could replace the CA proxy — trained against real local reports instead of assumed boundary conditions, still bounded by the same missing drainage-capacity data as any hydraulic model.
